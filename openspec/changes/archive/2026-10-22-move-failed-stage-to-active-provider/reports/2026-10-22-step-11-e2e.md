# Step 11: end-to-end test in the browser (JOS-172, move-failed-stage-to-active-provider)

Date: 2026-10-22. Branch `feature/jos-172-move-failed-stage`. Driven with Chrome (the Claude in Chrome tools, not Playwright MCP). The backend ran on port 3192 with the launcher of step 10 (two stub adapters per stage, `stub-a` failing on demand, a scratch store and key file, `ADMIN_PASSWORD` set) and the real frontend ran on Vite at port 5173 with `VITE_API_BASE=http://127.0.0.1:3192`. Port 3100 was in use by another process and was left alone.

## 11.1 Applicability

A new `/admin` section: the step applies.

## 11.2 The list

Two sessions were prepared: "E2E voice" (its voice stage failed on `stub-a`, retries exhausted) and "E2E clip" (both clips failed on `stub-a`), with `stub-b` made the active voice and video provider. Without the password `/admin` shows only the password form. After unlocking, the "Failed stages" section lists three rows: "Video of E2E clip, scene 1", "Video of E2E clip, scene 2" and "Voice of E2E voice", each "bound to stub-a, active provider stub-b", with its cause and a button "Move … to stub-b".

## 11.3 Move, then retry from the session page

- Clicking "Move voice of E2E voice to stub-b" showed "Moved. Retry it from the session page." with an "Open session" link to `/?sessionId=…`, and the voice row left the list. The store showed the voice binding `stub-b`, the failure still present and no request sent to `stub-b`.
- On the session page the stage still showed "Failed" with "Retry voice-over". The first click did nothing because the privacy prompt (opt-in telemetry) was open over the page; after dismissing it with "Not now" the click sent the retry. The voice-over completed and the session went on to decomposition, scenes and the final video.
- The attempts: four `stub-a` attempts before the move (`initial` and three `automatic`) and one `stub-b` attempt (`manual`, cycle 2); the call log shows one `voice stub-b` call. The diagnostics read "Voice-over: Unknown provider, 5 attempts" because `stub-a` and `stub-b` are ids no profile names (the display name of a real provider shows as in JOS-218).

## 11.4 A moved clip

A video provider "Short clips" (admitted 5 to 7 s, with a key) was added and made active. In the list, "Move video of E2E clip, scene 2 to Short clips" moved the clip. The scene details of scene 2 then read "Requested duration 7" and "Speed factor 1.3738" (it was 10 s and 1.04 before the move), and its clip line read "Clip: Short clips". Scene 1, not moved, kept 7 s and 1.05.

## 11.5 Restore

Both servers were stopped, the scratch folder was deleted and ports 3192 and 5173 are free. `backend/data/` does not exist, so the default store was untouched, and no `.secrets.json` was created.
