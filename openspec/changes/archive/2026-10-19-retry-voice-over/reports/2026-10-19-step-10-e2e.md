## Finding and fix (spec, design and tasks updated first)

On the first run the held retry was accepted by the backend (session read: `voice-over-generating`, voice-over `in-progress`, `heldCount` 1, no failure), but the page kept showing `Failed` and the Retry button. A held retry runs no step, so nothing published the cleared failure to live subscribers. `retryDecomposition` (JOS-156) hit the same gap and calls `broadcast`.

- Spec: new scenario "The accepted retry is published" in `voice-over-manual-retry`.
- Design Decision 7 and tasks (new 4.5): the command publishes the new state once the retry is accepted.
- Test first: the held case failed (no snapshot with the voice-over in progress); the sent case already passed.
- Fix: `retryVoiceOver` calls `broadcast(sessionId)` after releasing the attempts.
- Backend suite after the fix: 1702 tests passed. Then the browser run was repeated on a new failed, paused session and passed as in the table.

Voice-over attempt count on the page: 5 after the retry (4 failed, 1 successful), which matches the stored rows.
