# Scene registration

Requirements for turning the ordered fragments of a completed segmentation into the session's chunks (JOS-144, US-11; PRD §5 step 5, §6, §6.1). How the script is segmented, how narration intervals are assigned, and how a failed decomposition is retried belong to their own capabilities.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Chunks are numbered 1 to N in fragment order

The system SHALL assign each chunk a numeric identifier from 1 to N following the order of its fragment in the original script. The identifier SHALL be unique within the session and MAY repeat across sessions.

#### Scenario: A decomposition is registered

- **GIVEN** a session and N ordered fragments
- **WHEN** the chunks are registered
- **THEN** their identifiers are the consecutive integers 1 to N, in the fragments' order

#### Scenario: Two sessions are registered

- **GIVEN** two sessions, each with a registered decomposition
- **WHEN** their chunks are read
- **THEN** each session has its own chunk 1, and neither session's chunks appear in the other

#### Scenario: A duplicate number is written directly to the store

- **GIVEN** a session with a chunk numbered 1
- **WHEN** another chunk numbered 1 is written for the same session
- **THEN** the store refuses it

### Requirement: Every chunk carries its four content fields

Every registered chunk SHALL carry a non-empty `ID`, `PROMPT`, `IMAGE` and `VIDEO`. `PROMPT` SHALL be the fragment's text unchanged. `IMAGE` and `VIDEO` SHALL be generated by the reasoning provider from the fragments, and generating them SHALL NOT modify the script.

#### Scenario: Chunks are inspected after a registration

- **GIVEN** a completed registration
- **WHEN** the chunks are inspected
- **THEN** every chunk has a non-empty `ID`, `PROMPT`, `IMAGE` and `VIDEO`
- **AND** each `PROMPT` equals its fragment's text

#### Scenario: The instructions are requested

- **GIVEN** N valid fragments
- **WHEN** the visual instructions are requested
- **THEN** one request carries all N fragment texts in order
- **AND** the stored script is unchanged

### Requirement: An invalid decomposition is a decomposition failure

The system SHALL refuse to register a decomposition that is invalid, and SHALL record it on the session as a failure of the decomposition phase, attributed to the system and not to the User's script. A decomposition is invalid when it has no fragments, when a fragment's text is empty, when a fragment's narrated duration is outside the §6.1 bounds other than the §6.1.1 exceptions, when the fragments joined in order do not reconstruct the locked script apart from whitespace, or when the generated instructions are missing, empty, or not exactly one pair per fragment. A refused decomposition SHALL leave the session with no chunks.

#### Scenario: A fragment's duration violates the bounds

- **GIVEN** fragments where one lasts 3 seconds with no exception flag
- **WHEN** registration is attempted
- **THEN** no chunk is registered
- **AND** the session has a failure with phase `decomposition`
- **AND** the recorded cause does not attribute the failure to the User's script

#### Scenario: The §6.1.1 exceptions are accepted

- **GIVEN** either a single fragment below the lower bound flagged `script-below-lower-bound`, or a fragment above the upper bound flagged `unsplittable-sentence`
- **WHEN** registration is attempted
- **THEN** the chunks are registered

#### Scenario: The fragments do not reconstruct the script

- **GIVEN** fragments whose texts, joined in order, differ from the locked script beyond whitespace
- **WHEN** registration is attempted
- **THEN** no chunk is registered
- **AND** the session has a failure with phase `decomposition`

#### Scenario: The generated instructions are incomplete

- **GIVEN** a reasoning response with fewer instruction pairs than fragments, or an empty instruction
- **WHEN** registration is attempted
- **THEN** no chunk is registered
- **AND** the session has a failure with phase `decomposition`

#### Scenario: The reasoning provider fails

- **GIVEN** the reasoning provider answers with an error
- **WHEN** registration is attempted
- **THEN** no chunk is registered
- **AND** the session has a failure with phase `decomposition`, retryable unless the error is a client error other than 408 or 429

#### Scenario: The session reports the failure

- **GIVEN** a session whose decomposition failed and which has no chunks
- **WHEN** the session is read
- **THEN** its state is `failed` with failed phase `decomposition`

### Requirement: Established chunks cannot be split, merged, deleted or reordered

Once registered, a chunk's identifier, `PROMPT` and session SHALL NOT change and the chunk SHALL NOT be deleted. The store SHALL refuse these changes for every caller. No operation of the API or the session page SHALL offer to split, merge, delete or reorder chunks. A session that already has chunks SHALL NOT have a second decomposition registered.

#### Scenario: A chunk's number or prompt is changed directly in the store

- **GIVEN** a registered chunk
- **WHEN** its identifier, its `PROMPT` or its session is updated
- **THEN** the store refuses the update and the chunk is unchanged

#### Scenario: A chunk is deleted directly in the store

- **GIVEN** a registered chunk
- **WHEN** it is deleted
- **THEN** the store refuses the deletion and the chunk still exists

#### Scenario: The visual instructions stay correctable

- **GIVEN** a registered chunk
- **WHEN** its `IMAGE` or `VIDEO` instruction is updated
- **THEN** the update is stored

#### Scenario: The API and the session page are inspected

- **WHEN** the operations the API offers and the controls on the session page are listed
- **THEN** none of them splits, merges, deletes or reorders chunks

#### Scenario: A second decomposition is attempted

- **GIVEN** a session that already has chunks
- **WHEN** another decomposition is registered for it
- **THEN** it is refused and the existing chunks are unchanged

### Requirement: Registered chunks start in submitted and the session in chunks-processing

On a successful registration, every chunk SHALL be in state `submitted` and the session SHALL be in state `chunks-processing`.

#### Scenario: A registration succeeds

- **GIVEN** a valid decomposition
- **WHEN** its chunks are registered
- **THEN** every chunk is `submitted`
- **AND** the session state is `chunks-processing`
- **AND** the session representation lists the chunks in ascending identifier order with their `PROMPT`, `IMAGE` and `VIDEO`
