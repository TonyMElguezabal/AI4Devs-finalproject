## Defects found and fixed (spec first, then test, then code)

1. **A held retry was not published.** The first pass on session C showed the page still `Failed` after the click, while the store held a scheduled cycle-2 attempt. A held attempt runs no step, so nothing broadcast the cleared failure. Fix: `retryDecomposition` publishes the session state after releasing the attempt (design Decision 2, step 9; spec scenario "A held retry is published"; task 5.4; commit 861b802).
2. **No Continue control while decomposing.** After the fix the page showed `Waiting for you to continue (1 held)` with no way to continue: the header offered Pause and Continue only in `chunks-processing` and `final-video-generating`. Fix: `chunk-decomposing` joins them (design Decision 8; spec scenario "Continue a held retry from the page"; task 7.3; commit 28a7faa).

After both fixes 11.3 was repeated on a fresh session and 11.4 on a fresh session; both behave as above.

## Notes
