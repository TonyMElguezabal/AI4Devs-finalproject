# Step 12: E2E testing (JOS-190, adjust-scene-pause)

Date: 2026-10-15. Tool: `playwright-cli` (Chromium). Targets were located by role and accessible name. The same harness as step 11 (real backend, real ffmpeg, fixture-seeded session, port 3299) with Vite on 5299 (`VITE_API_BASE=http://127.0.0.1:3299`).

## Scenarios

1. **Open the session, expand scene 2 (12.2).** The page showed the Final video section with `Download final video` and `Version 1`; scene 2's details held the spinbutton `Pause after scene 2` = 1, a disabled `Reset scene 2 pause`, and "From 0.2 s to 3.0 s, in steps of 0.1 s. Originally 1.0 s."
2. **Set the pause (12.2).** Filling `Pause after scene 2` with 0.4 and pressing Enter kept 0.4, enabled `Reset scene 2 pause`, and made `Rebuild final video` appear beside `Version 1` — all without a reload.
3. **Rebuild (12.3).** Clicking `Rebuild final video` led, without a reload, to `Version 2`, with the Rebuild button gone and `Download final video` still present.
4. **Refusal (12.4).** Entering 3.5 showed, as a status, "The pause after scene 2 can be 0.2 s to 3.0 s, in steps of 0.1 s, or its original 1.0 s."; the field returned to the saved 0.4.
5. **Reset (12.4).** `Reset scene 2 pause` restored 1 and disabled itself. `Rebuild final video` stayed (version 2 was built with the 0.4 s trim, so returning to the original is a pending change), which is the intended behaviour.
6. **Phone width, dark (12.4).** At 375 px `scrollWidth` equalled `innerWidth`; the dial, its Reset and the range sentence stacked cleanly.

## Defect found and fixed during this step

Console showed **two** 422 responses for one Enter. Enter saved and disabled the field, which blurred it, and `onBlur` saved again (on a successful save this was a duplicate POST). Fixed with a single-flight guard in `PauseDial`; the new test fires the blur explicitly and was confirmed to fail without the guard and pass with it (jsdom does not blur a field that becomes disabled, so a plain `user.click` does not reproduce it).
