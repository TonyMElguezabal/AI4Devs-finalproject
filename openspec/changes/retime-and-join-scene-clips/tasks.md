## 3. Assembly plan (TDD, pure; design Decisions 1 and 3)

- [ ] 3.1 Failing tests in `assembly-plan.test.ts` for ordering: scenes supplied 3, 1, 2 come out 1, 2, 3
- [ ] 3.2 Failing tests for each refusal: a gap, a last end short of the voice-over, a missing index, a duplicate index, a clip duration of 0, NaN and negative, and a scene left with zero frames; each reason names the scene
- [ ] 3.3 Failing tests for frame arithmetic: the fixture's intervals at 30 fps give frame counts summing to 432; 200 intervals of 2.71 s give every start within 1 frame of `start × 30` and a sum of `round(542 × 30)`; a half-frame interval end (design Risk on `round`) yields the documented `Math.round` result
