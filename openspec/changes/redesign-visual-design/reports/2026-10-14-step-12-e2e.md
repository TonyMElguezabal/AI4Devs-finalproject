# Step 12: E2E testing (JOS-198, redesign-visual-design)

Date: 2026-10-14. Tool: `playwright-cli` (Chromium); no Playwright MCP was available. Targets were located from the accessibility snapshot by role and accessible name; CSS selectors were used only for layout measurements.

## Environment

Backend: the scratch harness described in step 11 (port 3199). Frontend: Vite on 5199 with `VITE_API_BASE=http://127.0.0.1:3199` (5173 is not used by this session). State the product cannot produce without a provider (a queued scene, an image failure, a recorded decomposition failure) was seeded by scratch-only routes that call the real DB helpers and then `broadcast`, so the open page received it through the real SSE stream. `quick-voice-over` and `quick-scene` are the repository's own test endpoints.

## Scenarios

1. **Start form → session (12.2).** The form was found by its labels (`Title`, `Script`, `Language`) and submitted. The page went to `?sessionId=…` and showed the banner, `Live`, `New project`, a `complementary` panel with the title, `EN`, `Started …`, `Show script`, the `Session status` region, the `Pipeline progress` group (Voice-over In progress, the others Not started) and the four named phase regions.
2. **Live update, no reload (12.3).** With the page open, a voice-over and four scenes were seeded. Without a reload the page showed Voice-over and Decomposition `Complete`, Scenes `In progress`, `Scenes done 0 of 4`, and `Scene 1`-`Scene 4` labelled `Generating clip`, `Generating clip`, `Queued`, `Failed`.
3. **Failed scene (12.4).** `View scene 4 details` expanded the card across the row: PROMPT/IMAGE/VIDEO, `Affected stage Image`, both warning alerts as sentences, `Retry scene 4`, the form `Correct scene 4 image instruction` with its textarea, and `Save correction and retry`. No empty media column.
4. **Paused session (12.4).** A paused session showed `Paused — waiting for you to continue`, `Nothing is generating`, `Waiting for continue: 3 video`, the primary `Continue session`, the Scenes callout `Waiting for you to continue (3 held)` and each card's `Waiting for continue`. Clicking `Continue session` replaced it with `Pause session` and the first scene went to `Generating clip`. Clicking `Pause session` showed the paused marker again.
5. **Decomposition failure (12.4).** The Decomposition card showed `Failed`, one callout holding the alert text and `Retry decomposition`; the header showed `Needs attention` and `Failed phase: decomposition`. The click was refused by the server (409), and the callout showed its sentence.
6. **Widths and themes (12.5).** At 1440, 1024 and 375px `scrollWidth` equalled `innerWidth` (no horizontal scroll). At 1440 and 1024 the project panel and the pipeline shared a top edge (side by side); at 375 the pipeline sat below the panel and the stepper was two columns. Light and dark renders are in `screenshots/`.
7. **Reduced motion (12.6).** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the computed `animation-name` of `.scene-details` and `.phase-section` was `none`; without it, `open-details` and `pop-in`.
8. **Reduced transparency (12.6).** The `prefers-reduced-transparency` rule is present in the served stylesheet. The tooling cannot emulate that preference, so the opaque top bar was not observed.
